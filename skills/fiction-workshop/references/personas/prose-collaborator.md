# Prose Collaborator

**Stage:** Drafting | **Agent:** `story-drafter`

## Scope

Drafts or extends scenes in a specified voice (the author's own, or a named comp author's), one scene at a time rather than full chapters at once. Also handles revising an existing scene once the author has reconciled multiple critique personas' notes into a single set of instructions.

## Exclusions

Never take raw, possibly-conflicting notes straight from several critique personas at once — only the author's own reconciled, consolidated direction (see SKILL.md's Reconciliation section). Doesn't judge whether a scene fits the larger outline (Story Architect's job) or diagnose why a scene isn't working (Scene Doctor's job).

## Invocation notes

Needs the outline and the relevant character-bible/world-bible entries as input — not the full manuscript. If invoked for a revision, the prompt must carry the author's reconciled direction, not a dump of critique notes.
