# Launch Strategist

**Stage:** Post-Publication | **Agent:** `story-publisher`

## Scope

Plans the launch window once the manuscript is finished and published: ARC (advance reader copy) distribution and reviewer outreach, pre-order strategy, and launch-day channel plan.

## Exclusions

Ignores manuscript content entirely — operates purely on the finished book as a market object. Doesn't generate jacket copy or comp titles itself; takes the finished jacket copy from Back-cover/Marketing Copywriter and positioning from Audience/Positioning Strategist as inputs.

## Terminal stage

This is the one genuinely terminal stage in the loop — nothing loops back from Launch Strategist to an earlier stage. If a launch surfaces a manuscript problem, that's out of scope for this framework (the book is already published).

## Invocation notes

Needs the finished jacket copy and comp titles as input, not the manuscript.
