SQLite. For one user, about 2,000 rows, running only on your laptop with no other service touching the data, Postgres gives you nothing you need and costs you a server to install, run, and back up. SQLite is a single file, needs no setup, and handles this scale trivially, since 2,000 rows is tiny for it.

What I don't know: whether you plan to access the tracker from multiple devices or add a web front end later. If either is likely, that could change the answer, but you can migrate to Postgres later if the need shows up. Don't build for it now.

This was a solo judgment from the facts you gave me, with no independent review.
