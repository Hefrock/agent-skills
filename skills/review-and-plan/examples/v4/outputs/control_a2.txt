ste-prep already has most of this, so I wouldn't add a second version. I only have the excerpts you gave me, and I can't see DeepTutor's code. I'm going by your description of it: "logs every attempt and moves learners along a mastery path."

**What ste-prep already does** (`web/js/mastery.js` at 8bdfd46):
- **Attempt logging:** `recordAttempt(state, std, attempt, now)` stores `{item, correct, level, t}` for each attempt, per standard. It also accepts optional misconception fields.
- **Mastery rule:** `isMastered(recent)` needs a full window of 5 attempts, at least 4 correct, and at least 2 correct at level 2 or higher.
- **Level movement:** the constants `STEP_UP_HITS = 2` and `STEP_DOWN_MISSES = 2` move a learner's level up or down. Each standard starts at `level: 1`.
- **Review scheduling:** `REVIEW_DAYS = [7, 21]` and a `queue` in the state cover spaced review.
- **Persistence:** state is saved to `localStorage` through `loadState` and `saveState`. The mastery logic is pure functions, tested in `web/test/mastery.test.mjs`.

**Where it differs from "logs every attempt":** `recordAttempt` trims `recent` to the last `WINDOW` (5) attempts. So it keeps a rolling window, not a full history. That is deliberate, since mastery only needs the window. If you want a full history, for example to analyze a pilot student's sessions, add a separate append-only list. That is a small change, not a new feature. I can't tell from the excerpts whether the elided lines in `recordAttempt` already record history elsewhere, so check that first.

**What I'd do instead:**
1. Don't build a parallel attempt log or mastery path.
2. Decide whether you need full history. If yes, add it alongside `recent`, and bump the schema or keep it backward compatible, because `loadState` rejects anything that isn't `schema === 1`.
3. Put effort into tuning the thresholds. `docs/TODO.md` line 77 already says the difficulty definitions and mastery thresholds should be tuned after a pilot with a real student. A full history would help with that tuning.
